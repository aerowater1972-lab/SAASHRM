# Pricing FAQ

## General

### How is pricing calculated?
Pricing is **per employee per month**. You pay for active employees only (ONBOARDING, ACTIVE). Inactive (RESIGNED, TERMINATED, OFFBOARDED) not charged.

### What's included in the per-seat price?
All features for your chosen plan, hosting, backups, security updates, support per your plan's SLA.

### Are there setup fees?
No setup fees for Starter/Professional. Enterprise includes implementation in annual fee.

### Can I change plans mid-contract?
- **Upgrade**: Anytime, pro-rated charge
- **Downgrade**: Only at renewal (30 days notice)

### What happens if I exceed seat count?
Auto-upgrade at next billing cycle. 5-seat grace period (14 days).

## Billing

### Payment Terms
| Plan | Billing Cycle | Payment Methods |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| Starter | Monthly / Annual | Bank Transfer, Credit Card |
| Professional | Annual | Bank Transfer |
| Enterprise | Annual (3 yr) | Bank Transfer |

### Payment Schedule
- **Annual**: Upfront, due Net 14
- **Monthly**: Advance, due 1st of month
- **Late Fee**: 1.5%/month after 30 days overdue

### Invoices
- Generated 1st of month (monthly) or anniversary (annual)
- Sent via email + available in Admin -> Billing
- Include: Seat count, plan, add-ons, PPN (11%)

### Tax (PPN)
- All prices exclude 11% PPN
- PKP registered companies: Provide NPWP for e-Faktur
- Non-PKP: PPN still applies (no input credit)

- **14-day free trial** (Professional features, up to 20 employees)

|------|------|--------------|

- **Monthly**: Cancel anytime, effective end of billing period
- **Annual**: 30 days written notice before renewal

You own all data. Flexy HRMS is processor; you are controller.

- **Self-serve**: Admin -> Settings -> Export (JSON/CSV/PDF)
- **On request**: Full dump (SQL dump + files) within 30 days
