## FAQ

**Q: Can I upgrade/downgrade mid-contract?**
A: Upgrade anytime (pro-rated). Downgrade at renewal.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: 14-day trial (Professional features, up to 20 employees).

**Q: What payment methods?**
A: Bank transfer (monthly/annual), Credit card (via Stripe).

**Q: Data ownership?**
A: You own your data. Export anytime (JSON/CSV/PDF).

**Q: Data residency?**
A: Indonesia (Jakarta DC) standard. Singapore/EU on Enterprise.

**Q: Integrations?**
A: API + Webhooks (Pro/Ent), Pre-built: Slack, Teams, GSuite, Xero, Jurnal, Mekari.
